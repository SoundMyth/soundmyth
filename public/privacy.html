Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

BPM Radar (“we”, “us”) is a free web app that lists upcoming electronic music events. This policy explains what personal data we process, why, and what rights you have.

1. Who is responsible

Data controller: [FULL NAME], Spain. Privacy contact: [PRIVACY EMAIL].

2. What we collect

You can use BPM Radar without an account. Browsing events requires no personal data from you. If you create an account (login by email link) we process:

  • Your email address, to send you the login link and identify your account.
  • An optional display name.
  • Your preferences (home cities, favourite DJs and styles), your saved events and the date you completed onboarding, so they sync across your devices.
  • Your choice about email recommendations and offers and the date you made it.

A profile photo you choose stays on your device; we do not upload it. We do not ask for your location, phone number or payment details.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies, so there is no cookie banner. Your browser’s local storage holds only what the app needs to work: your login session, favourites, preferences and a cache of events for offline use. Usage statistics come from Vercel Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and stores no personal data: it counts page views and anonymous visitors using a short-lived hash that is discarded after 24 hours, plus country, device, browser and referrer. We strip query strings before sending.

6. How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account is active and deleted when you ask us to delete it. Email consent records are kept while the account exists. Aggregate analytics hold no personal data.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time (email preferences can be changed in Account). To delete your account or exercise any right, write to [PRIVACY EMAIL] from the address you signed up with. We reply within one month. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

BPM Radar is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell account holders by email.
